# Ecosystem possibilities - product roadmap, NOT current Skill capabilities

The NAS is a private, durable home for source files, approved chapters and future intentions. The supported Round 1 Skill uses only the cataloged photo, file, document and knowledge expert families.

Potential future UGREEN ecosystem touchpoints (subject to separate hardware/API availability, permissions and user research):

1. **Phone / portable SSD / docking workflow:** Voluntary import of a selected batch of family media into an authorized NAS location before invoking the Skill. The Skill does not control phone sync or UGREEN docks.
2. **Memory Window / family display:** Show only a household-approved chapter or anniversary story on a separate display interface. No display pairing, casting or automatic surfacing is implemented.
3. **One-button Memory Capture:** A hypothetical push-to-record accessory could collect one expressly initiated account with visible recording status and speaker consent. No microphone, voice assistant or hardware controls exist in this Skill.
4. **Opt-in moment discovery:** A future app might suggest one or two user-approved recent moments per month. No background watching, camera integration, notification, facial recognition or automatic scan is implemented.
5. **Letters to the Future / time capsules:** Long-term access management, recipient permission inheritance, backups, reminders and timed release would require separate mechanisms and verification; a `save-text` operation does not create them.

**Positioning:** The Missing Chapter turns user-confirmed memories into meaningful future experiences people freely choose to create: revisit cherished times, complete a missed moment, carry forward a tradition or create something new from an old insight. It is an explicit-intent tool, not a prediction, surveillance or automated family-management product.

6. **Caring Moments surface (future UX):** On an opt-in phone/home interface, someone may ask about a birthday, graduation, daily gratitude or a gentle family ritual. The actual Round 1 Skill only responds to user initiation; no automatic trigger, calendar or background NAS scanning exists.
7. **A relationship-centered home ecosystem (future UX):** A family-approved display could feature a birthday memory, a phone could voluntarily capture a response, and a future push-to-record accessory could preserve the voice of a willing storyteller. All would require documented device APIs, recipient visibility, approvals and deletion/data-retention controls. No hardware interoperability is currently claimed.

**Frequent relevance, not frequent interruption:** The product ambition is a quiet, trusted companion people open whenever they want to care for somebody. Ordinary Tuesdays matter as much as milestones.
