# Purposeful Unwritten Chapters - the meaningful-future protocol

## Product thesis

**Some memories deserve more than remembering. They deserve another chapter.** The Skill does not predict the future or compile a generic bucket list. It helps a user choose a future experience that carries emotional purpose from *their own* remembered past. The source of meaning must be user-authored and a real memory anchor must be confirmed; otherwise offer a sparse unfinished draft.

## Five optional pathways (choose, mix, or decline)

1. **Give a missed moment another chance:** A chosen opportunity to spend time, say something, or revisit an experience that did not unfold as the user hoped. Do not assume guilt, grief or an obligation to reconnect; respect safety and other people's boundaries.
2. **Revisit what mattered:** Return to a place, family ritual or shared activity while acknowledging circumstances and people may have changed. Preserve the old story without trying to recreate it exactly.
3. **Finish a postponed chapter:** An unfinished creative project, postponed adventure or promise *the user* still wants to explore. Never assign deadlines or success metrics without request.
4. **Carry something forward:** Pass a recipe, story, craft, language, practice or value to a willing family member; do not assume descendants must participate.
5. **Create from a past insight:** Turn a former turning point, challenge or joyful discovery into a fresh experience, collaboration or act of care. The AI asks, not declares, the lesson.

These are **user-selectable lenses**, not automatic conclusions inferred from media. A photo never proves a regret, estrangement or unfulfilled aspiration.

## Meaning Bridge - must be explicit

Record the following in the user's own words or note the field as unconfirmed:

- **Memory anchor:** one real past event, person, relationship, ritual or insight from approved source(s) (`DOCUMENTED`) or attributed user recollection (`USER_TESTIMONY`); source references only if actually returned.
- **What mattered / remains open:** cherished element, unfinished thread or learning *as described by user*. May be positive, bittersweet or simply inspiring.
- **Why now:** optional reflection, not AI-imposed urgency or mortality framing.
- **Chosen next chapter:** a specific future lived experience, freely chosen, not model prediction.
- **Agency and consent:** another person's presence is a wish, not a command; no pressure, covert invitations or messages.
- **Meaningful outcome:** what would make the moment valuable even if it unfolds differently than expected. No score/guarantee.

**Purpose Check:** Does the user recognize the memory-to-future link? Is the purpose theirs? Is it respectful of everyone else's choice? If not, ask once and keep draft open; never invent rationale.

## Output template: My Unwritten Chapter

**Title:** The Recipe We Have Yet to Make Together (fictional example only)
**Life stage:** `UNWRITTEN`  **Record state:** `DRAFT`
**Purpose lens:** `CARRY_FORWARD` (only if chosen by user)
**Memory anchor + evidence:** A confirmed memory, cited if applicable; or `User testimony only - no NAS archives consulted`
**What this memory means to me:** Attributed user statement, not AI sentiment analysis
**What I hope to create:** A future shared experience the user actually chooses
**Why this next chapter matters:** User-authored connection to memory
**People and boundaries:** Only voluntarily mentioned participants, with consent assumptions disclosed
**Optional small step:** Blank unless user requests suggestions; no scheduling
**What actually happened:** *Blank - to be completed after the user returns*
**How it felt / what changed:** *Blank - no simulated future memories*
**Source scope:** Exact approved sources / partial coverage / no archive used
**Review / destination:** Draft until exact content, path, filename, audience and workflow approval are confirmed

## State and later return

- `Life stage: UNWRITTEN` means a stated hope, not an event that happened; `LIVED` requires a new, explicit user account after the event.
- `Record state: DRAFT|APPROVED_SAVED` tracks whether an actual new text file was saved and returned verified completion evidence.
- Keep the initial aspiration intact. A later user-initiated Return to a Chapter reads the exact authorized path (or user-provided text), puts **"What I hoped for"** beside **"What actually happened"**, and creates a separate reviewed version. What happened can differ or remain unfinished; do not classify that as failure.
- An optional future letter is an approved ordinary text document. It does **not** implement delivery scheduling, timed access, confidentiality escrow, inheritable permissions, real-time devices or notifications.
