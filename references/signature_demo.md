# Signature demo - “The Sundays You Gave Me” (fictional test fixture)

Purpose: one **memorable, feasible, evidence-led** demonstration for live testing, not a fabricated real-customer story.

## Authorized synthetic test assets
- A fictional family photo showing two people by a bicycle. At demo time select only the test album and present the actual returned file path/date confidence; **do not claim identities from the image**.
- A short user-authored fictional statement that identifies participants and says they repaired bicycles on Sundays.
- No actual family data, minors' personal files, outside APIs, scripted runtime, purchases or messages.

## Two-minute narrative spine
**0:00 - The relatable need** — “Dad turns seventy. I don't want another generic present.” Skill responds with one gentle question and offers to look at **one selected album only**.

**0:20 - Honest discovery** — Registered photo-agent returns a real path for an old bicycle photo. The Skill says only what the file supports: “I found a bicycle photo. What does it mean to you?” No invented date or facial identity.

**0:45 - The human meaning** — User confirms: “That's Dad and me. Every Sunday he taught me to repair bikes. Those afternoons made me feel important.” The Skill attributes that meaning to the speaker. Its in-session graph links person, memory, meaning and today's occasion, with evidence labels.

**1:10 - Not another product recommendation** — Moment Card shows: A) photo-and-coffee conversation, B) repairing a bicycle together *if he wants and is comfortable*, C) a short reviewed illustrated letter. The Skill asks: “Would he still enjoy this, or would the conversation mean more now?”

**1:35 - A choice with agency** — User chooses the photo letter and a short coffee visit. The Skill drafts a personal message, labels the plan `PROPOSED`, offers edit/reject, and asks for content+workflow approval before any new save. If permissions block a save, show an unsaved draft and real error.

**1:55 - The future is not fabricated** — Explain: “After his birthday, you can tell me what actually happened, and we can add a new chapter.” No automatic notification, claimed event completion, or assumed emotional response.

Closing thought: **“Some gifts are not things we buy. They are moments we make time for.”**

## Required on-NAS evidence for a real video
Capture authentic agent results and valid file references, show the selected album scope, user correction, meaningful option, decision, write approval, and completed/failed status. A storyboard alone is not runtime proof. Do not make a before/after video implying successful NAS execution until tested.

## Qualities demonstrated
- **User value:** concrete gift/experience; one next step.
- **Differentiation:** source → user meaning → chosen action (not photo search alone).
- **Technical quality:** exact scope, provenance, error/status and approval.
- **NAS fit:** genuine local archive search and approved text preservation through expert agents.
